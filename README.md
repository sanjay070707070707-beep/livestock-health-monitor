# Smart Livestock Health Monitor

A Smart India Hackathon 2026 software solution for early detection,
prevention, and management of livestock diseases and animal health issues.

## Problem Statement

**SIH26128 — Government of Maharashtra**

Efficient systems for early detection, prevention, and management of
livestock diseases and animal health issues.

The system helps connect livestock health reporting, risk assessment,
geospatial monitoring, vaccination tracking, mortality reporting, and
outbreak detection in one platform.

## Key Features

- Livestock registration and management
- Animal health record management
- Temperature and symptom based health assessment
- Java-based ML risk prediction
- Healthy / At Risk / High Risk classification
- High-risk alerts
- Mortality reporting
- Vaccination tracking
- Village-level outbreak detection
- Geospatial livestock surveillance map
- Health history for individual livestock
- Field veterinarian and para-veterinary reporting
- MySQL database
- Web-based dashboard
